Getting started: 1) Turn the ignition ON (engine running or not). 2) Make sure the car's head unit is not connected to the adapter — it only allows one connection. 3) Pair the adapter once in Windows Settings → Bluetooth & devices (PIN is usually 1234 or 0000). 4) Click Connect and pick the adapter (or "Standard Serial over Bluetooth link (COMx)"). If nothing is listed, try Connect BLE. Click Demo to try it without the car.
